# Synthetic transcripts

The pilot transcripts identify respondents and their firms, so they are not in this repository. These three transcripts were written for it instead. They follow the structure of the pilot calls (a host who opens the call, an interviewer, one respondent; open questions; owner-run firms) and include the kinds of passages the method has to handle: a force raised without prompting, one raised after the interviewer suggests it, one described only for other firms, and a core topic (AI) that the interviewer reaches late in one call. No person or firm in them is real, and no sentence is taken from a pilot transcript.
